Planning & packages

Are these real packages I can book?

No. These are sample packages in a client showcase. Their prices, inclusions, and itinerary routes demonstrate a travel-agency website. A real agency would confirm suppliers, dates, terms, and total cost before accepting a booking.

Can a package be customised?

The demo quote builder lets you vary travellers, rooms, stay style, and a transfer allowance. The custom inquiry form covers other needs. These changes generate illustrative planning figures, not supplier commitments.

What is included in a sample package?

Each package page lists illustrative accommodation, activity, breakfast, and transfer allowances separately from exclusions. Flights, travel insurance, unlisted permits and charges, and optional activities are excluded unless a real proposal explicitly includes them.

How are children and rooms priced?

The sample calculator uses the displayed child rate and a two-adults-per-room assumption. Extra room capacity has an illustrative single-occupancy allowance. Real hotel and operator child-age, bedding, and occupancy rules can be different.

Are accessibility or dietary needs supported?

Use the custom inquiry notes to explain planning requirements without sharing sensitive medical details. Suitability, accommodation access, transport, and meals must be checked with the actual provider before confirmation.

Bookings, payments & changes

Does creating a reference confirm my booking?

No. References beginning IP- are local demo request records. They do not reserve rooms, transport, or activities. A real booking needs a confirmed proposal and the agency’s agreed confirmation process.

Can I pay with GCash, Maya, cards, or a bank transfer?

The showcase presents these as payment preferences to demonstrate a checkout journey. No payment gateway is connected, no payment account is supplied, no card details are collected, and no money is taken.

Can I cancel or change a request?

My demo requests lets you try change and cancellation actions on local records. No operator is notified and no real arrangement is cancelled. A real agency’s applicable supplier terms and written agreement determine changes, charges, and refunds.

What happens if weather changes the itinerary?

Routes may change with weather, sea conditions, local restrictions, or supplier availability. A real provider should explain rescheduling and cancellation terms before booking. Check official advisories and follow operator safety advice.

Saved data & support

Where are my saved trips and requests stored?

Saved trips, inquiry drafts, checklist progress, and demo request records stay in local storage in this browser. They do not sync across devices and are removed when you clear the site’s browser data.

Is the staff workspace private?

No. It is a public, labelled client demonstration using local browser records and optional synthetic samples. It has no authentication and must never be used as a private live staff system. Use sample customer details only.

Does the inquiry form send emails?

The supplied receiving address is a placeholder, so sending stays unavailable. You can review, save, and download a request. After a real inbox is configured and activated, the separate send action can submit through FormSubmit.

Is IdliPulo DOT-accredited?

No accreditation is claimed by this website concept. A real agency launch needs verified business details and any applicable credentials. Check claims using official Department of Tourism channels.

How can I use this website for my own agency?

Contact Casa Rustia Digital Services to discuss branding, real package data, agency details, approved policies, and the live services your operation needs. The website footer links to Casa Rustia.
