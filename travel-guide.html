A little preparation. More room for adventure.

Before the bag is packed.

Useful questions, official sources, and a checklist to help you move from daydreaming to a better-informed plan.

01 / Before you commit

Know who you are booking with.

Check the agency’s business identity, contact details, and claimed credentials. Verify accreditation through the Department of Tourism’s official channels rather than relying only on a logo or screenshot.

Department of Tourism accreditation portal ↗

02 / Before you travel

Check your documents at the source.

Entry, visa, identification, and passenger requirements depend on nationality, route, carrier, and current rules. Check official guidance before booking and again before departure. Do not upload passports or identity documents to this showcase.

  • Bureau of Immigration
  • Official Philippine eTravel portal
  • Department of Foreign Affairs consular services
03 / Island connections

Give the journey breathing room.

Flights, ferries, airport transfers, and island excursions involve separate providers and conditions. Check baggage allowances and pickup details. Leave room between connections and avoid treating a sample itinerary as a transport timetable.

Keep operator contact details, booking confirmations, and the agreed pickup location somewhere accessible offline.

04 / Weather & flexibility

A flexible plan is a better companion.

Sea conditions, rainfall, and local access rules can affect tours and transfers. Check forecasts and official advisories, follow your operator’s safety guidance, and ask about rescheduling before paying.

PAGASA weather information ↗

05 / Travel with care

Leave room for local knowledge.

Use suitable local guides, follow community rules, ask before photographing people, and respect wildlife and marine areas. Keep reusable water bottles and sun protection handy, and take litter back with you.

06 / A clear proposal

Read the details before agreeing.

Confirm room occupancy, meals, transfers, guides, permit fees, taxes or charges, cancellation terms, payment deadlines, and the point when a booking becomes confirmed. Ask who handles changes and refunds if an airline or supplier changes the plan.
