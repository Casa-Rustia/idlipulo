Ask about your trip ↗
Can I change a suggested itinerary?

Yes. The routes are starting points. Share your interests, dates, and pace in the trip request so a proposal can be shaped around you. No hotel, tour, or transfer is confirmed by choosing an itinerary.

How much will my trip cost?

Costs depend on dates, group size, accommodation, transport, and activities. Share a comfortable budget when you prepare your request. A final proposal should itemise inclusions, exclusions, and terms before you agree to anything.

Are flights and transfers included?

They are not automatically included. Request the arrangements you need, and confirm each inclusion in your proposal. Allow enough time between flights, ferries, and transfers.

What happens if the weather changes?

Sea conditions, weather, local access rules, and operator availability can affect routes. Keep plans flexible and confirm arrangements with your operator. Ask about cancellation and rescheduling terms before booking.

Can I plan a trip with children or a group?

Yes, you can include adults, children, and any accessibility or comfort needs in your request. A suitable route and activities need to be checked for your group before confirmation.

Does this website take payments or confirm bookings?

No. IdliPulo is a travel website showcase by Casa Rustia Digital Services. You can explore itineraries, save favourites, and prepare a travel request. A confirmed booking requires a separate agreement with a travel provider.
