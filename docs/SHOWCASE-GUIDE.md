# IdliPulo client presentation

Use sample names and emails. No real booking, availability check, payment, email, or supplier contact is made by the demo quote workflow.

## A complete traveller-to-agency story

1. Open the homepage and explain the dual-tone/dual-weight IdliPulo wordmark and responsive design.
2. Explore the six Philippine destinations. Use a category filter, save a destination, and open its itinerary.
3. Open **Packages**. Filter by destination, trip length, or style. Choose two or three packages and use **Compare selected**.
4. Open an individual package. Show the itinerary, illustrative inclusions/exclusions, and itinerary download.
5. Use **Build my sample quote**. Change adults, children, rooms, stay style, and the transfer upgrade. The PHP planning estimate updates immediately.
6. Continue with sample traveller details. Review the estimate, try a payment-method preview, acknowledge the demonstration, and create a demo request.
7. Download the brief and open **My demo requests**. Show the reference and request history, then try a change request.
8. Open **Staff workspace demo** from the footer. Find the same local request, change its pipeline status, add a sample follow-up note, and save.
9. Return to **My demo requests** to show that the status and history have updated.
10. In the staff view, load synthetic sample records to demonstrate a larger pipeline. Search/filter records and export JSON.
11. Show agency services, the configurable contact profile, travel preparation checklist, searchable FAQs, and booking information.
12. On a phone-sized viewport, demonstrate the mobile navigation, package filters, quote form, and horizontally scrollable staff table.

## Sample calculation

El Nido package, 3 adults, 1 child, 2 rooms, Boutique stay, private-transfer upgrade:

- Adults: 3 × PHP 18,900 = PHP 56,700.
- Child: 1 × PHP 14,175 = PHP 14,175.
- Single-occupancy allowance: 1 spare adult bed × PHP 700 × 3 nights = PHP 2,100.
- Boutique allowance: 2 rooms × PHP 1,800 × 3 nights = PHP 10,800.
- Transfer upgrade: PHP 1,500.
- **Illustrative total: PHP 85,275.**

These are demonstration assumptions, not travel-provider rates or policies.

## Keep the distinction clear

- A request reference is browser-only, not a supplier confirmation.
- The staff workspace is deliberately public and unauthenticated.
- The payment preview does not collect payment details or issue receipts.
- Saved trips and requests do not sync across devices.
- The normal inquiry form still requires a real configured inbox for email delivery.
- No accreditation, client reviews, office address, or airline/hotel partnership is invented.

## Reset after presenting

Use **Clear local demo records** in the staff workspace to clear its sample requests. Use the privacy page’s **Clear IdliPulo data on this device** to remove requests, saved destinations, inquiry drafts, and checklist progress. The reset affects only this browser.
